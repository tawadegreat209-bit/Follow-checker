How do I get my Instagram data?
  1. Instagram app: Settings, then Accounts Center.
  2. Your information and permissions, then Download your information.
  3. Choose "Followers and following", format JSON, date range "All time".
  4. Wait for the email, download the zip.
  5. Upload the zip below (or the followers_1.json and following.json files from the connections folder).
